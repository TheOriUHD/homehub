## Gotchas learned

- Paper ≤1.16.5 refuses Java 17+; Paper 1.13.2 wants positional `nogui`.
- A Forge jar's `loaderVersion` floor must be the oldest Forge major it
  claims, not the one it was built on.
- The auto-mode classifier blocks reading the DB URL from the systemd unit;
  rolled-back `psql` transactions and the service journal are the substitutes.

## Addendum, 15:30 — Telegram alerts and the assistant (`b13d984`, `8442305`)

Owner's complaint: "ask it about the printer alert and it has nothing", and
"printer's back" followed two minutes later by "down again".

Findings, from the thread rows and the journal, not from the code:
- Alerts *were* appended to the thread, but the model sees the newest 30 rows
  and a flapping network had filled them with alerts; the printer alert was
  long gone and none carried a time.
- The back/down-again pairs were HomeHub restarts (13:30 and 14:46 — mine).
  The status collector's recovery hold is in memory; a restart forgets the
  fault, the recovery goes out at once, and the re-fire passes the 30-minute
  floor because "warning" ranks above "recovered".

What changed: `internal/api/api_notices.go` owns the whole path now (gate →
narrator → deliver). Recoveries park for 10 minutes in
`signal_notices.recovery_due_at`, flushed by the sweep, withdrawn if the
fault returns; recoveries for unreported faults are dropped; every sent
message is in `signal_notice_log`; the narrator sees the last 30 minutes of
it. Thread rows for alerts carry a `NoticeMarker` in `tool_trace` and are
rendered `[Alert you pushed Tue 25 Aug 14:47] …`; the prompt Context carries
the clock; the `sent_notifications` tool returns the log with each
condition's standing now.

Live check after deploy: the network healed (the modded server came back on
26.2) and both recoveries were parked, not announced (journal 15:07:24). The
first flush then dropped them wrongly — `GetLiveSignal` also returns a
resolved row, because the store reuses one undismissed row per key — fixed
in `c64f2d5` with `HasLiveSignal`; re-parked by hand, they went out at
15:21 as "Aperture NVR's back to normal, Sir. That camera issue cleared
about 13 minutes ago and hasn't come back." and "AcardiaMC's back up, Sir.
All six servers connected again — was down to five for a bit over forty
hours." Both true, both in `signal_notice_log` and in the thread with
their marker.

Not done: the narrator's phrasing was not re-probed against the live model
this session (the classifier blocks the DB-URL probe pattern); the prompt
changes are additive and unit-tested. Watch the thread for a day.
